# AI Governance Toolkit

Simple, free templates for managing AI in a company. Each tool is a
spreadsheet or document you can download and fill in.

## What's inside

1. **AI System Inventory**: a list of every AI system your company
   uses, with owners, risk ratings, and review dates.
   (folder: 01-ai-system-inventory)

More tools are coming, including a risk rating guide and an AI
impact assessment.

## Frameworks used

NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ISO/IEC 42001, EU AI Act
